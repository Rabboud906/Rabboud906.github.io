Personal project
Oct 2026
Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe

AirCanvas: draw in the air with your hand

A real-time computer vision app that turns a webcam into a painting surface. It finds your hand, reads which fingers are up, and treats that as a tool: one finger draws, two fingers pick colors and brush size, an open palm erases, and a fist pauses.

MediaPipe's Hand Landmarker gives 21 landmarks per frame from a 1280×720 feed. I wrote the gesture classifier myself, using joint angles and fingertip-to-wrist distances, then stabilized it so a single noisy frame cannot flip the tool or scatter stray ink.

Strokes are stored as operations and drawn onto a color layer with an alpha mask, then blended over the live video. That is what makes undo, redo, and a pixel-clean eraser possible.

Tools
Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, NumPy
Methods
Hand landmark tracking, geometric gesture classification, temporal smoothing, alpha-mask compositing
Controls
Draw, select, erase, pause, undo and redo, brush size from 2 to 40 px, whiteboard mode, PNG export
Known limits
Accuracy drops when the hand is partly hidden or leaves the frame, and gesture angles shift with hand orientation

Team project

Jul – Aug 2026
Remote team project

Automated bottle inspection

A virtual inspection station that looks at a bottle image and decides PASS or FAIL. I was Project Manager and Modeling Lead, so I ran the plan and built the model.

The system pairs classical image measurements, which show why an image was flagged, with a ResNet-18 classifier fine-tuned through transfer learning. Every inspection returns a decision, a confidence score, the measurements behind it, and an annotated image.

Once it worked, we changed the lighting and added blur and noise to see how much each one hurt accuracy. That told us where the system could be trusted and where it could not.

Methods

92% accuracy on unseen images
